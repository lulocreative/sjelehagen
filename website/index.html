Meet Cecilie, the woman behind Sjelehagen

“For me, the road here began with a wish to feel better in my own body.”

In my teens and twenties I had a strained relationship with my body and food, and experienced hormonal imbalances and inner restlessness. Wanting to understand my body better led me to Ayurveda, yoga and a whole-body approach to health.

I started Sjelehagen because I missed a place that showed me how to train and live in tune with my body, and get results without wearing myself out.

Got a question?

Is this suitable for beginners?+

Yes. Every class is labelled by level, and the Calm & flow track is made for you if you’re starting from zero or coming back after a break.

What happens after the trial?+

You’ll get an email reminder before the 7 days are up. If you cancel before day 8, you pay nothing.

Do I have to log my cycle?+

No, it’s optional. Without cycle data you get suggestions based on how you feel that day. If you’re pregnant, in menopause or don’t menstruate, you can follow the moon phases instead.

Do I need equipment?+

A mat is enough. Some sculpt classes use light weights or bands, but everything can be done without.

How do I cancel?+

Under My page, in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
