Sjelehagen Retreats

Slow down. Breathe by the sea.

The online studio fits into everyday life. A retreat is the opposite: five days away from it, to move, eat, rest and reconnect without the to-do list. It’s the Sjelehagen way of living, in person.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need experience?+

No. Every session has options for beginners and for those who train regularly. Many guests come to slow down, not to push.

Can I come on my own?+

Yes, most guests do. The small group makes it easy to connect, and there is always time to be by yourself.

What does it cost?+

The price is announced together with the dates and venue. Waitlist members get the early-bird price first.

Can you handle allergies or special diets?+

The food is plant-based by default. Tell us about allergies or needs when you book, and the kitchen will adapt.

Is travel included?+

Flights are not included. Once the venue is confirmed we share the nearest airport, transfer options and travel tips.
